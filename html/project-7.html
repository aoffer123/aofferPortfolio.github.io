Technical project lead and developer

WRM / Southwaste Payment Portal

A multi-brand customer payment portal serving more than 200,000 accounts.

Year

2025 to 2026

Tech & technique

C# / .NET SQL Sage ERP integration REST APIs Dynamic multi-brand theming

Overview

WRM/Southwaste needed a payment portal that could serve four distinct customer-facing brands from one platform. I led the project as both technical project lead and lead developer, from discovery through launch, working across the front end and back end.

Customers could view balances, pay invoices, and access statements online, with branding customized automatically based on a customer's account code, so each of the four brands, logos, emails, and statements, matched the provider the customer actually knew, across a base of over 200,000 accounts.

Key features

  • Dynamic per-customer branding across four distinct brands
  • Online invoice payment
  • Account balance and statement viewing
  • Sage ERP data sync

Technical highlights

  • Built a dynamic branding system that re-skins the entire experience, logo, colors, emails, and statements, based on which of the four brands a customer's account code belongs to
  • Synced customer and billing data with the client's Sage ERP system to keep the portal accurate for over 200,000 accounts
  • Automated past-due and upcoming-payment email notifications and generated downloadable PDF statements

My role

  • Technical project lead and lead developer, owning delivery from discovery through launch and post-launch support
  • Worked across the full stack, front-end UI and back-end logic, including the ERP sync
  • Coordinated requirements, demos, QA/UAT, and go-live directly with the client
