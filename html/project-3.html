New Leaf

A habit-tracking app built around replacing bad habits, not just counting streaks.

Year

2022

Tech & technique

JavaScript SQL Material 3 Figma

Overview

Most habit-tracking apps are good at visualizing streaks and completion, but they don't actually help anyone break a bad habit, bad habits get reinforced immediately, and most trackers offer no mechanism to interrupt that. New Leaf approaches habit change differently: instead of just counting streaks, it encourages users to replace a bad habit with a good one, using personalized reinforcement, categorization, goals, and custom rewards to keep the substitution sticking.

As the front-end developer and UI/UX designer, I owned the interface from paper wireframes through a connected, working app, not just a prototype.

Key features

  • Habit creation and categorization
  • Goal setting and custom rewards
  • Notifications and progress tracking

Technical highlights

  • Designed the interaction model around habit replacement rather than simple streak-counting
  • Built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma using the Material 3 component library before writing production code
  • Connected the front end to a real SQL-backed server rather than a static prototype

My role

  • Acted as both front-end developer and UI/UX designer for the app
  • Took the design from paper wireframes through high-fidelity Figma prototypes
  • Presented prototypes to stakeholders and incorporated feedback before implementation
