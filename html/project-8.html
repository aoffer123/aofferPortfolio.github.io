Overview

Opus Inspection runs vehicle safety and emissions-testing programs for government agencies around the world. I worked as lead front-end developer on their subscription billing portal, giving inspection-program customers a self-service way to manage recurring billing, view invoices, and make payments.

As with the WRM/Southwaste project, this meant working across both ends of the stack, the customer-facing billing UI and the backend logic tying it to Opus's customer accounts.

Key features

  • Invoice and payment history
  • Account management for inspection-program customers
