Local Lots

A hyperlocal, identity-verified marketplace for community resale and bidding.

Year

2025

Tech & technique

Remix TypeScript Tailwind CSS C# / .NET SignalR PayPal Plaid

Overview

Local Lots is a community bidding platform built on a simple idea: your neighbors want what you're done with, so keep the deal local instead of shipping to a stranger. Every member verifies their identity through Plaid before they can buy or sell, and contact information stays private until a PayPal payment clears, so nobody has to share an address with someone they haven't transacted with yet.

I led front-end development and UX across the whole buying and selling flow, from browsing live auctions through scheduling a preview, placing a bid, and checking out.

Key features

  • Live auction discovery and bidding, with real-time price updates
  • Lot detail pages with preview scheduling
  • Plaid identity verification
  • PayPal-secured checkout with privacy-first contact sharing

Technical highlights

  • Used SignalR to push real-time bid updates to everyone watching a live auction, so prices update instantly without a page refresh
  • Integrated Plaid for identity verification and PayPal for secure payment before contact info is exchanged
  • Contributed to the C#/.NET backend supporting bidding and transaction workflows

My role

  • Lead front-end developer, owning UX for discovery, lot details, bidding, account management, and checkout
  • Shaped the responsive layout and interaction design across desktop and mobile
  • Worked with the backend team on the PayPal and Plaid integrations end to end
