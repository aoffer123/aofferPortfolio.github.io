UI design and development

Keystone Fleet, Mobile

A mobile-first redesign of in-vehicle key pairing and fleet operations.

Year

2023 to 2024

Tech & technique

React Material 3 ProtoPie SCSS

Overview

Alongside the web dashboard, the Keystone mobile app needed the same modernization, redesigned for the realities of in-vehicle use: vehicle connection, key pairing, and advanced operations screens that needed to work reliably on a phone, often with someone standing next to a vehicle rather than at a desk.

The redesign moved from low-fidelity paper sketches through interactive ProtoPie prototypes before landing on a shipped Material 3 interface, with review cycles that pulled in product, development, sales, operations, and marketing to make sure the design held up across every team that touched it.

Key features

  • Redesigned vehicle connection flow for faster, clearer pairing
  • Streamlined key pairing experience
  • Simplified advanced operations screen for in-field use

Technical highlights

  • Took the redesign from low-fidelity paper sketches through interactive ProtoPie prototypes that closely simulated the final Material 3 interface
  • Ran stakeholder review cycles across product, development, sales, operations, and marketing
  • Handed off high-fidelity designs and interaction specs to the development team for implementation

My role

  • Owned UI design and prototyping for the mobile app redesign
  • Sketched and tested low-fidelity flows before investing in high-fidelity ProtoPie prototypes
  • Worked closely with the development team to translate approved designs into the shipped Material 3 interface
